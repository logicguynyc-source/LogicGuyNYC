My Approach

Many LSAT companies make the test way more complicated than it needs to be. Said companies collect millions of dollars, and in the process they cause thousands of young Americans to fall victim to our nation's ongoing loneliness and mental health epidemic.

My job is to help my clients develop simple, effective strategies that they can apply to pretty much every LSAT problem they confront! Did you know, for example, that all problems in the logical reasoning section can be categorized as one of three easy-to-understand question types? Or that you can diagram an "unless" claim the exact same way you diagram an "or" claim? Or that basically every question whose stem calls the passage "information" (rather than an argument) is a must-be-true question in disguise? Nothing makes me happier than watching LSAT students who were once intimidated by the test learn how to ace it!

During the first few weeks of lessons, I introduce a few core strategies that are designed to help students improve their test pacing, their process of elimination, and their attention to detail. I then give my students homework assignments designed to solidify these habits and strategies, so that they quickly start to feel like second nature. I tend to be a very encouraging and enthusiastic tutor; more than anything, I want students to feel more confident in their own intelligence and test taking ability after working with me. On test day, my tutees go into the exam room confident that they have all the tools they need!
